Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Starfield Cosmic Chess (starfieldcosmicchess.com)

Starfield Cosmic Chess is a Pi-native social strategy platform built around minimal data collection and verified human interaction inside Pi Browser.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in via Pi Authentication, we collect only your Pi UID and Pi username, along with your in-app match history, cosmic faction allegiance, sportsmanship reputation badges, and active match chat messages. We do not collect emails, phone numbers, real names, or financial credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to provide matchmaking, server-validated chess gameplay, real-time board synchronization, community reputation tracking, and human-first safety moderation.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell Pioneer data to third parties. All database writes are validated server-side, external links are blocked inside match chat to protect against phishing, and Firestore access is restricted by strict security rules.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Commander profile and associated match records at any time through the in-app Safety & Support report tool.
