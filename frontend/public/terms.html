Terms of Service & Community Rules

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Starfield Cosmic Chess (starfieldcosmicchess.com)

Welcome to Starfield Cosmic Chess. By accessing this platform inside Pi Browser, you agree to uphold a safe, fair, non-speculative community for verified Pioneers.

1. Free Social Strategy — Zero Wagering

Starfield Cosmic Chess is strictly a free-to-play social strategy community. Gambling, wagering, betting, cash-out schemes, token farming, and pay-to-win advantages are strictly prohibited.

2. Pioneer Code of Conduct

  • Respect Every Pioneer: Harassment, hate speech, slurs, and personal insults are prohibited.
  • Play Honorably: Do not use external chess engines or intentionally stall clocks during matches.
  • Keep the Galaxy Scam-Free: Never ask for Pi Wallet passphrases or post external URLs in match chat.
  • Use Safety Tools Constructively: Mute opponents in 1 tap or submit a report for human moderator review.

3. Human-First Moderation

Violations reviewed by human moderators may result in educational warnings, temporary chat mutes, or account suspension to protect the Pi community.
